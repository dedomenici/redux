# Kaohsiung Redux shortlist

Ranked by how iconic the scene is and how easy the place is to use in public. Coordinates and sources are in `taiwan_film_locations.csv`, and every pin is also on the app's near-you page (https://dedomenici.github.io/redux/near — type Kaohsiung).

1. **Huangpu New Village, Fengshan** () · 22.61783, 120.36427  
   Scene: Detention 返校 (2019): 1960s martial-law dorms and streets; also 鬥魚, Mr. Long 龍先生 and 四月望雨  
   Why: Open, preserved first-generation military village opposite the Military Academy, with streets you can walk freely. Easy Redux of the Detention corridors and alleys, or the Mr. Long noodle-stall life.  
   Sources: https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=119 · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=134 · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=272
2. **Dragon and Tiger Pagodas and Spring & Autumn Pavilions, Lotus Pond, Zuoying** (蓮池潭龍虎塔、春秋閣) · 22.68051, 120.29238 and 22.68225, 120.29283  
   Scene: The Wayward Cloud 天邊一朵雲 (2005): musical number 奇妙的約會 at the pagodas (watermelon-umbrella dance); Tsai said he chose the Spring & Autumn Pavilions for the 'sweet love' musical numbers. The 1959 comedy 王哥柳哥遊台灣 also used the pavilions. A shorter non-musical scene was shot on the nearby lakeside steps (approximate).  
   Why: Free public landmark, very photogenic. A musical number is a perfect participatory Redux. Further *Wayward Cloud* pins sit about 3 km south at Riverside Park and its Light Bridges (including the loop-shaped 指環橋), where Shiang-chyi finds a watermelon and the couple reunite. The apartment interiors in 河堤社區 were not pinned — the exact building is unidentified.  
   Sources: https://www.kff.tw/content/2020-11 · https://takao.kcg.gov.tw/article/1461 · https://www.walkerland.com.tw/article/view/80448
3. **Love Pier** (真愛碼頭) · 22.61926, 120.28943  
   Scene: Black & White 痞子英雄: the “South District Police Station” of Harbour City; also Uma 酷馬 and Kingfisher 魚狗  
   Why: Public waterfront where the Love River meets the harbour. Cop-show swagger, chases and stand-offs.  
   Sources: https://takao.kcg.gov.tw/article/2856 · https://uptogo.com.tw/%E5%A8%9B%E6%A8%82/%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1/%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1-%E9%AB%98%E9%9B%84%E5%8F%96%E6%99%AF%EF%BC%9F/
4. **Singuang Pier** (新光碼頭) · 22.60951, 120.29704  
   Scene: Black & White 痞子英雄: the cash-van explosion  
   Why: Big open pier. Stage the explosion with cardboard and people as the fireball.  
   Sources: https://uptogo.com.tw/%E5%A8%9B%E6%A8%82/%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1/%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1-%E9%AB%98%E9%9B%84%E5%8F%96%E6%99%AF%EF%BC%9F/ · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=122
5. **Love River by Wufu Bridge, beside the Kaohsiung Film Archive** (愛河五福橋) · 22.62361, 120.28972  
   Scene: Eight Hundred Heroes 八百壯士 (1975): Brigitte Lin swims the flag across “Suzhou Creek”, really the Love River  
   Why: Right next to the Film Archive, a natural workshop base. The flag swim can be restaged on the bank.  
   Sources: https://takao.kcg.gov.tw/article/1670
6. **Qixian 3rd Rd, Yancheng (approximate, street-level)** (興華街) · 22.63056, 120.28423  
   Scene: The Boys from Fengkuei 風櫃來的人 (1983): the Penghu boys are conned into paying to “watch a film” from an empty building's rooftop  
   Why: Hou Hsiao-hsien classic. Do the con scene at street level. The rooftop is private.  
   Sources: https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=295 · https://www.nownews.com/news/2447508
7. **Cijin ferry (Gushan to Cijin)** (旗津渡船頭) · 22.61391, 120.26976  
   Scene: The Boys from Fengkuei, Three Times 最好的時光, No Puedo Vivir Sin Ti 不能沒有你  
   Why: A public ferry ride used by three acclaimed films, with lots of action and big crowds.  
   Sources: https://www.walkerland.com.tw/article/view/80448
8. **Shoushan Zoo** () · 22.63452, 120.27515  
   Scene: BIG (2023, Wei Te-sheng); also Hotel Saltwater and The Zoo (atlas)  
   Why: Public zoo. Family-friendly participatory scenes.  
   Sources: https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=461
9. **Pier-2 Art Center** (駁二藝術特區) · 22.61994, 120.28168  
   Scene: Midnight Diner (Chinese version) 深夜食堂; Black & White seaside bar; 命中註定那頭鵝 meet-cute on Dayong Rd  
   Why: Public creative park with warehouses. Easy permissions via the city.  
   Sources: https://uptogo.com.tw/%E5%A8%9B%E6%A8%82/%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1/%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1-%E9%AB%98%E9%9B%84%E5%8F%96%E6%99%AF%EF%BC%9F/ · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=287
10. **Ciaotou Sugar Refinery** () · 22.75793, 120.31412  
   Scene: The Bold, the Corrupt, and the Beautiful 血觀音 (2017, Golden Horse Best Film): the manager's residence; also 江湖無難事 and Hotel Saltwater  
   Why: Public heritage park on the MRT Red Line, with colonial-era houses and a gothic family drama.  
   Sources: https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=117 · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=217 · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=289
11. **Guomao Community, Zuoying** () · 22.67004, 120.28798  
   Scene: Mr. Long 龍先生 (2017, SABU), The Scoundrels 狂徒, （真）新的一天, 我和我的賽車老爸  
   Why: A striking curved 1980s housing estate in public streets, very cinematic.  
   Sources: https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=137 · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=272 · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=457
12. **Fengshan Gymnasium** () · 22.62115, 120.35469  
   Scene: The Great Buddha+ 大佛普拉斯 (2017); Midnight Diner  
   Why: Indoor venue that needs permission, but the film is beloved.  
   Sources: https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=274 · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=287
13. **Cijin Beach** () · 22.61018, 120.26692  
   Scene: Incantation 咒 (2022, Taiwan's biggest horror hit); Father and Son 父子關係 (1986)  
   Why: Public beach. Horror Redux on the sand.  
   Sources: https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=122 · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=295 · https://www.filmkh.org/tc/teams_detail?tm_sn=296
14. **Chaofeng Temple viewpoint, Dagangshan (Alian)** (大岡山超峰寺觀景台) · 22.85482, 120.34226  
   Scene: 18×2 Beyond Youthful Days 青春18×2 (2024): the night-view scene  
   Why: Further out, around 40 minutes by car. A good night shoot.  
   Sources: https://uptogo.com.tw/%E5%A8%9B%E6%A8%82/%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1/%E9%9B%BB%E5%BD%B1-%E9%AB%98%E9%9B%84%E5%8F%96%E6%99%AF%EF%BC%9F/

Note: 明德訓練班 (Mingde Training Camp, used in Detention) is a strong scene, but filmkh lists it as under restoration and not available.
